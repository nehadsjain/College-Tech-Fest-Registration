About

College Tech Fest 2026 is an exciting technical event organized to bring together students from different colleges to showcase their talents, creativity, and technical knowledge. The event includes coding competitions, workshops, project exhibitions, quizzes, and expert sessions, providing participants with an opportunity to learn, innovate, and collaborate.

Objectives

  • Enhance students' technical and problem-solving skills.
  • Encourage innovation, creativity, and teamwork.
  • Provide a platform to showcase projects and ideas.
  • Promote learning through competitions and workshops.
  • Connect students with industry experts and professionals.

Highlights

  • 💻 Coding Competition
  • 🚀 Hackathon Challenge
  • 🤖 Project Exhibition
  • 🎤 Expert Guest Sessions
  • 🧩 Technical Quiz
  • 🏆 Exciting Prizes and Certificates
